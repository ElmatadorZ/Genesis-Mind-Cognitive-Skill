**These facts would decide it:**

1. **What changes, and for whom.** New price points, tiers, billing model (per-seat, usage, flat), and whether current customers move to it or keep their old pricing.
2. **Unit economics.** Margin per customer under the old and new plans, and how many customers you could lose before revenue falls.
3. **What customers value.** Is the new price tied to the thing they actually pay for? Or is it tied to something that's easy for you to measure but means little to them?
4. **Competitive anchor.** What alternatives cost, and how easily customers can switch.
5. **The goal.** More revenue per customer, more new signups, fewer cancellations, or a better-fitting mix of customers. A plan can only be good or bad against a goal.
