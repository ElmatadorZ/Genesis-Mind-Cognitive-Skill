I can't judge it yet because I haven't seen the plan. Whether it's a good idea comes down to a few facts:

**What decides it**
1. **What changes and for whom.** Is it a price increase, new tiers, usage-based billing, or a cut? Which customers does it hit: new, existing, or a particular segment?
2. **Your unit economics.** Does the new price cover cost to serve and acquisition cost with margin to spare? Or does it fix a revenue problem by creating a churn problem?
3. **Willingness to pay.** Do you have evidence (tests, sales calls, competitor prices) that customers see enough value at the new price? Or is it an internal guess?
4. **What the plan is meant to do.** Grow revenue per customer, win more customers, push people to a higher tier, or simplify? A plan can be "good" for one of these and bad for another.
5. **Rollout and reversibility.** Are existing customers grandfathered? Can you test it on a segment before going all in, and roll it back if needed?

**Ask first**
- What problem is the new plan solving, and how will you know in 90 days if it worked?
- Have you tested it on real customers, or only modeled it?
