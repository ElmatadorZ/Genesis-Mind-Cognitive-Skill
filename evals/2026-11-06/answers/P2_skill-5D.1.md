**Challenge:** If the Thai market you can realistically reach is small, or you are already near its ceiling, staying put limits growth and weakens the next raise. Some investors in Southeast Asia want a regional story.

**Challenge:** You have about 8 months before fundraising has to start. A new market rarely shows a clear signal in under 6 to 9 months.
**Truth:** A full Vietnam launch probably cannot show results before you need to raise. The decision has to fit inside that window.
